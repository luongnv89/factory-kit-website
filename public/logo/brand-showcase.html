A bounded workflow. A visible approval boundary. An identity built for evidence, not automation spectacle.

Design concept

Work moves. Approval stays separate.

Workflow spine
A stable F silhouette with squared horizontal rails.
Detached stage
A small lower segment evokes inspectable, bounded work.
Approval gate
A distinct endpoint, separated by an explicit gap.

The geometric F ties the name to a staged engineering workflow. It is an abstraction, not a diagram of every implementation state.

The green gate is intentionally detached. Progress does not imply permission to merge. The human boundary remains visible even in one color.

Cream paper, near-black ink and restrained signal green retain the existing editorial identity. IBM Plex Mono Medium supplies a precise, developer-native wordmark without leaning on machine imagery.

The lowercase name and literal hyphen are preserved. All lockups share the same outlined letters and unmodified mark paths.
